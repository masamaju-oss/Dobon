## 再現プロンプト

```
Playing card back design, portrait 2:3. Deep navy-blue ground covered with a fine
engraved grid of thin vertical and horizontal lines. A thin double gold line frame
inset from the edges, with ornate gold foliate flourishes in the four corners.
In the center, a large circular gold medallion: a laurel wreath ring around a
sculpted relief of a single human eye in antique gold. The eye is cold and
watchful — heavy upper eyelid, the iris engraved with fine radial lines, a solid
round black pupil, and NO highlight in the eye. Small scroll ornaments join the
wreath at the top, bottom and sides. Antique, solemn, hardboiled; gold on deep
navy; no text, no letters.
```

## 要件メモ

- メダリオンは大きめに取る。集中線は入れない（地は縦横の細かい彫り線＝格子）。
- 目は冷徹に。ハイライト無し、虹彩と瞳孔はあり。
- この金の目が「目」モチーフの原本。目アイコン等はここから派生させる。
